## What I built

An automated workflow that produces SEO-ready blog articles from a topic — it
researches, drafts, and structures the piece so a human only has to review and
polish rather than write from scratch.

## How it works

The workflow is built as a **LangGraph** agent in **Python**, using
**Anthropic** models for the writing. Framing it as a graph of steps — research,
outline, draft, structure — keeps each stage focused and the output consistent,
rather than asking one prompt to do everything at once.

## Why it matters

Content production is a genuine bottleneck for a lot of teams. Automating the
heavy lifting removed **80% of the manual writing effort**, turning a blank page
into a solid draft and leaving people to do the part they're actually good at:
judgement and editing.
