## What I built

A SaaS platform that automates the document-heavy parts of a law firm's work —
the reading, sorting, and processing that sits between a case arriving and
someone being able to act on it. It was my final-year project, built end to end.

## How it works

Documents are read with **OCR** and processed by **OpenAI** models to pull out
what matters, exposed through a **FastAPI** backend in **Python**. The goal was
to compress the slow, manual first pass on every case into something automatic.

## Why it matters

Legal work is full of high-value people doing low-value document handling.
Automating that first pass cut **case processing time by 60%**, giving the firm
back the hours that used to disappear into paperwork.
